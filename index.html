Our story

North Indian & Pakistani home cooking, made for busy lives

Dawat Halal Meals is a freshly cooked daily meal delivery service serving the Greater Toronto Area. Our food is prepared in a fully equipped commercial kitchen with a certified food handler on premises, using refined vegetable oil.

Our aim is simple: every working professional and student should be able to come home to, or open at their desk, a proper home-style halal meal.

FAQ

Good to know

Still have a question? We're a message away.

Call us Email
Which days do you deliver?

Monday to Friday. There are no deliveries on Saturday or Sunday, so choose a weekday as your start date.

When will my plan start?

You choose your start date at checkout. Orders placed after 6 PM Eastern are processed the next day.

How do the subscriptions renew?

Weekly plans (5 meals) renew every 7 days. Monthly plans (20 meals) renew every 28 days. Manage your plan anytime from My Account.

How is the food packaged?

Meals come in microwavable, BPA-free containers. Thalis use a 3-compartment tray designed to be eaten on the go.

How can I pay?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, and cash on delivery.

Is delivery included?

Yes. Delivery to your front door, back door or condo lobby is included. Delivery to your apartment door is $1.99 per day.

Can you accommodate allergies?

Please note any allergies at checkout. Our meals commonly contain dairy, eggs, nuts and gluten, and we cannot guarantee against cross-contamination, so we can't accommodate severe dairy, egg, nut or fish allergies. See our terms.

Can I get a refund?

Because every meal is cooked fresh to order, we don't offer refunds on food items. Try a 1–4 day trial first if you're unsure.
